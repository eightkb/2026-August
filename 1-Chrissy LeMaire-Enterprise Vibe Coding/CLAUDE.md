# CLAUDE.md

## Style

- No two-beat aphorisms in slide titles or copy (e.g. "Markdown in. Issues out."). Don't use that shit.
- No "Do X, not Y" constructions (e.g. "Put your standards in hooks, not in docs"). Say what to do and let it stand on its own.
- No "That's the failure mode. Now name the practice." setup-pivot structures.
- Do not use these terms or phrases: blast radius; golden path; paved road; safety net; zero trust; surface area; seam; escape hatch; leaky abstraction; sharp edge; footgun; boundary; contract; invariant; idempotent; deterministic; declarative; slice; source of truth; single pane of glass; cognitive load; backpressure; circuit breaker; graceful degradation; resilience; temporal coupling; robust; production-ready; production-grade; future-proof; extensible; composable; anti-corruption layer; strangler fig; holistic; end-to-end; rigor; rigorous; critical path; leverage; de-risk; unblock; quick win; low-hanging fruit; north star; ADR; decision record; shift left; move the needle; first-class; opinionated.
